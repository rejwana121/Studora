# BUSINESS VISION

> AI-powered academic productivity and workload-intelligence startup

FINAL COMPACT EDITION  |  Student-only B2C: Free, Pro & Premium

## 1. Executive Vision

Vision: To become a trusted intelligent academic companion that helps university students organize academic responsibilities, complete important work on time and maintain sustainable study habits.

Mission: Combine academic task management, planning, workload intelligence, personalized recommendations and productivity analytics in one accessible mobile experience.

| Business concept: Studora is a direct-to-student freemium startup. Students use a useful Free plan and may purchase Pro or Premium monthly/annual subscriptions for advanced intelligence, automation and analytics. |
| --- |

## 2. Problem and Opportunity

Students manage assignments, quizzes, labs, projects, presentations, midterms, finals and study sessions across disconnected tools. Deadline clustering, poor effort estimation and delayed action can create excessive academic workload. Generic task apps record deadlines but rarely explain workload feasibility or recommend recovery actions.

- One academic workspace reduces fragmentation.
- Explainable workload-risk analysis creates differentiation.
- Repeated semester needs support recurring engagement.
- A Free plan can earn trust before subscription conversion.
## 3. Product and Student Value

| Capability | Student value |
| --- | --- |
| Tasks & deadlines | Capture and organize academic responsibilities |
| Planner & focus | Turn commitments into scheduled study actions |
| Workload intelligence | Identify excessive workload patterns early |
| Personalized guidance | Prioritize, split, reschedule or take a break |
| Dashboard | Measure progress, study time and workload trend |

| Safety boundary: Studora detects workload-related stress risk; it does not diagnose stress, anxiety, depression or any medical condition. |
| --- |

## 4. Target Students

| Segment | Need |
| --- | --- |
| Busy undergraduate | Clear daily priorities across several courses |
| First-year student | Guided planning habits |
| Final-year student | Coordination of projects, presentations and exams |
| Working student | Feasible planning within limited time |
| Productivity-focused student | Advanced analytics and personalization |

## 5. Value Proposition

For university students facing fragmented work and overlapping deadlines, Studora is an AI-powered academic assistant that converts tasks and study behaviour into a realistic, prioritized plan. Unlike generic task managers, it explains workload risk and recommends specific corrective actions.

- Academic-specific task types and semester context.
- Workload feasibility, not deadline display alone.
- Priority, task-splitting, rescheduling and break guidance.
- Colourful, calming and student-friendly experience.
- Privacy, transparency and non-diagnostic language.
## 6. Subscription Business Model

| Plan | Core value | Revenue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | Tasks, calendar, planner, timer, basic reminders and limited workload insight | No charge |
| Pro | Higher limits, full factors/history, smart planning and advanced analytics | Monthly/annual |
| Premium | Highest fair-use AI, automation, deep insights and priority support | Monthly/annual |

The MVP will demonstrate plan comparison, monthly/yearly selection, sandbox checkout and entitlement changes without collecting payment information or charging money. Live app-store payments are post-MVP.

## 7. Business Intelligence

| Perspective | Key measures |
| --- | --- |
| Student | Pending/completed/overdue, on-time rate, study time, workload trend |
| Product | Activation, weekly meaningful planning users, adoption and retention |
| Subscription | Paywall views, plan selection, sandbox conversion, future renewal/cancellation |
| Economics | Cost per active/paid user and margin by plan |

North-star metric: Weekly Meaningful Planning Users - students who manage academic work and complete at least one planning, study or recommendation action in a week.

## 8. Go-to-Market and Operations

- Pilot with a small student community; use ambassadors, referrals and student-focused content.
- Provide self-service onboarding, clear support and feedback paths.
- Show transparent subscription terms and cancellation rules.
- Protect personal data; no advertising or sale of academic/behavioural information.
- Monitor false alerts, product quality, cloud/AI cost and student retention.
## 9. Roadmap and Success

| Stage | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Discovery | Validate problem and willingness to use |
| Working MVP | Demonstrate end-to-end student value and sandbox business model |
| Student pilot | Measure usability, retention and recommendation value |
| Paid validation | Validate Pro/Premium willingness to pay |
| Public beta & growth | Launch live subscriptions and improve acquisition/retention |

| MVP success: A student can organize tasks, plan study, receive explainable workload-risk guidance and alerts, complete work, view updated metrics and demonstrate Free/Pro/Premium sandbox entitlement without a real charge. |
